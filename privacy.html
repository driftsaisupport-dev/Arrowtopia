Arrowtopia privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Arrowtopia ("the game") is made by driftsai (satyam rasal), city pen and country india. This page explains what information the game uses. The game is for everyone, including children, so we keep data to the minimum.

No account, no personal data collected by us

There is no sign-up or login. We, the developer, do not collect your name, email address, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not run any server. Your progress (levels, coins, album, arrow styles, settings, the year of birth you enter and your agreement to these terms) is saved only on your device.

If Android backup is turned on, Android keeps a copy of this progress in your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That copy is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

Year of birth

The first time you open the game it asks for a year of birth. This stays on your device and is used only to choose the right experience and ad settings: under 13 (child), 13 to 17 (teen) or 18 and over (adult). It is never sent to us or to advertisers; only the resulting group decides how ads are requested.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the following from the device:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate general location
  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information about ad performance and crashes
PlayerHow ads work
Under 13Every ad request is tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, with a maximum ad rating of G (general audiences). Ads are not personalised and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown. Children see only a small banner: no full-screen, video or app-open ads. Links out of the game, sharing and rating are behind a parental check.
13 to 17Ad requests are tagged as under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised, and the maximum ad rating is T (teen).
18 and overNormal ads. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Data is sent in encrypted form. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by phone).

Children

A parent or guardian must agree to the terms before a child under 13 plays. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no chat, no user profiles and no way for players to contact each other. The weekly league contains only computer (AI) rivals that run on the device.

Parents can delete all of the game's data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and can contact us at the address below with any question.

Notifications

If the daily reminder is turned on (it is never offered to children), the game schedules one local notification a day on the device. No data is sent anywhere for this. It can be turned off in the game's Settings.

Security

The game's screens are part of the app and cannot load anything from the internet. Only Google's ads software and Google Play services use the network. The game can only open its own policy pages and its Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and the game asks players to agree again when the change matters.
